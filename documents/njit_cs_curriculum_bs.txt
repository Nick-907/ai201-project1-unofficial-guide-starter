B.S. in Computer Science - New Jersey Institute of Technology (2025-2026)

Academic Advisor: https://computing.njit.edu/advising
Total Credits Required: 120 minimum

FIRST YEAR

Semester 1:
- CS 100: Roadmap to Computing (3 credits)
- MATH 111: Calculus I (4 credits)
- ENGL 101: English Composition: Introduction to Academic Writing (3 credits)
- PHYS 111: Physics I (3 credits)
- PHYS 111A: Physics I Lab (1 credit)
- FYS SEM: First-Year Student Seminar (0 credits)
Term Credits: 14

Semester 2:
- CS 113: Introduction to Computer Science I (3 credits)
- MATH 112: Calculus II (4 credits)
- ENGL 102: English Composition: Introduction to Writing for Research (3 credits)
- PHYS 121: Physics II (3 credits)
- PHYS 121A: Physics II Lab (1 credit)
Term Credits: 14

SECOND YEAR

Semester 1:
- CS 114: Introduction to Computer Science II (3 credits)
- CS/IS/IT Elective 200 or above (3 credits)
- MATH 333: Probability and Statistics (3 credits)
- Science Elective (3 credits)
- History and Humanities GER 200 level (3 credits)
Term Credits: 15

Semester 2:
- CS 241: Foundations of Computer Science I (3 credits)
- CS 280: Programming Language Concepts (3 credits)
- IS 350: Computers, Society and Ethics (3 credits)
- COM 312 or COM 313: Effective Communication or Writing in the Workplace (3 credits)
- MATH 337: Linear Algebra (3 credits)
- YWCC 207: Computing & Effective Com (1 credit)
Term Credits: 16

THIRD YEAR

Semester 1:
- CS 288: Intensive Programming in Linux (3 credits)
- CS 332: Principles of Operating Systems (3 credits)
- Social Sciences GER (3 credits)
- CS 301: Introduction to Data Science (3 credits)
- CS 356: Introduction to Computer Networks (3 credits)
Term Credits: 15

Semester 2:
- CS 331: Database System Design & Management (3 credits)
- YWCC 307: Professional Dev in Computing (1 credit)
- CS Elective 300 or above (3 credits)
- CS 341: Foundations of Computer Science II (3 credits)
- CS 350: Intro to Computer Systems (3 credits)
- CS 351: Introduction to Cybersecurity (3 credits)
Term Credits: 16

FOURTH YEAR

Semester 1:
- CS 435: Advanced Data Structures and Algorithm Design (3 credits)
- CS 490: Guided Design in Software Engineering (3 credits)
- History and Humanities GER 300+ level (3 credits)
- CS Elective 300 or above (3 credits)
- CS Elective 300 or above (3 credits)
Term Credits: 15

Semester 2:
- CS 491: Senior Project (3 credits)
- Humanities and Social Science Senior Seminar GER (3 credits)
- CS Elective 300 or above (3 credits)
- General Elective (3 credits)
- CS/IS/IT Elective 200 or above (3 credits)
Term Credits: 15

Total Credits: 120

MINIMUM GRADES:
Students are expected to earn a grade of B or better in CS 100. Students are expected to earn a grade of C or better in all CS courses that serve as prerequisites in a sequence of courses.

CO-OP:
A GPA of 2.7 is required to enroll in co-op. Students may use up to 6 credits of co-op toward their general elective requirements.

KEY REQUIRED CS COURSES (core sequence):
CS 100 → CS 113 → CS 114 → CS 241 → CS 280 → CS 288 → CS 332 → CS 341 → CS 350 → CS 435 → CS 490 → CS 491

CS 288: Intensive Programming in Linux is taken in Third Year, Semester 1.
CS 280: Programming Language Concepts is taken in Second Year, Semester 2.
CS 332: Principles of Operating Systems is taken in Third Year, Semester 1.
CS 350: Intro to Computer Systems is taken in Third Year, Semester 2.
