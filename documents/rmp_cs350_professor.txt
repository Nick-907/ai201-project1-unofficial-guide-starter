ITANI SUMMARY:
Professor Abdul-Rahman Itani teaches CS 350 (Intro to Computer Systems) and CS 288 at NJIT and has an overall rating of 4.0/5. He is one of the highest-rated CS professors at NJIT and many students call him the best CS professor at the school. He is known for passionate, engaging lectures that build real understanding. The passing threshold in CS 350 is 45 — a D is considered passing. The strategy to pass is to read the textbook before lecture, attend lecture, and read the textbook again after. Attendance is mandatory. The exams are extremely challenging, often harder than the in-class material. Negative reviews (which receive many community downvotes) allege poor office hour behavior, but the majority of students report positive experiences when they put in effort. Compared to Sohn for CS 350, opinions vary — some prefer Itani for his engaging lectures, others prefer Sohn for his structured practice exams.

SOHN SUMMARY:
Professor Andrew Sohn teaches CS 350 (Intro to Computer Systems) and CS 288 at NJIT with an overall rating of 3.5/5. He is known for being passionate about the material and always coming to class smiling. CS 350 with Sohn is bucket-graded, meaning you don't know if you passed until the final grade drops — a student with a 67 may fail. The final exam is widely described as extremely difficult. Students who do the homework thoroughly and attend office hours report doing well, since exam questions are similar to HW and practice exams. Some students prefer Sohn over Itani because he provides practice exams; others find his accent difficult to follow and lectures unclear. The course itself is described as one of the hardest CS courses at NJIT regardless of professor.
