SUMMARY:
Professor Bassel Arafeh teaches CS 280 (Programming Language Concepts) at NJIT. With an overall rating of 1.8/5 and only 20% of students saying they would take him again, he is one of the lower-rated CS professors at NJIT. The most common complaints are monotonous lectures, low attendance (less than half the class shows up after a few weeks), heavy reliance on slides, and the Vocareum autograded platform being frustrating. Programming Assignments (PAs) and Study Assignments (SAs) are described as time-consuming. Exams test niche material from slides and are not closely aligned with the study guide. The course itself, CS 280, is not a programming course conceptually but still has heavy programming assignments, which surprises students. Some students note that the professor is not a bad person and that reading the slides can yield a passing grade. One student gave a very positive review praising his teaching as inspirational. If another professor is available for CS 280, students strongly recommend taking them instead.
