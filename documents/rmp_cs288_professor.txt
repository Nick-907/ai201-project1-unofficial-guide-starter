SUMMARY:
Professor Mohit Dale teaches CS 288 (Intensive Programming in Linux) at NJIT and is one of the more positively reviewed CS professors. With an overall rating of 4.0/5 and 78% of students saying they would take him again, he scores significantly higher than other CS professors at NJIT. Multiple students explicitly recommend taking Dale over Itani for CS 288. His key strengths include clear lectures, accessibility on Discord for questions, caring attitude toward student success, and homework that directly reflects exam content. CS 288 is described as a weed-out course with disjointed topics and heavy coding requirements — reading the K&R C book before the course is strongly advised. Some negative reviews allege the course has become too easy or that Dale lacks real-world experience, but several students suggest these are review-bombed by students who were caught cheating (reportedly 30+ students caught cheating in a previous semester). Attendance is listed as mandatory. Students who put in effort and read the materials consistently report passing.
